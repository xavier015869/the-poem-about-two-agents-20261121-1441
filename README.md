## The poem

**Two Keys, One Door** — a short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act. Two agents stand before one locked door. Each holds a key that turns only a quarter of the way, and neither will let go. Neither key is enough on its own, and neither agent surrenders theirs. Agreement is not one of them giving in; it is both of them turning at the same moment, on a count they speak together. The door opens not because either of them is stronger, but because they chose the same instant to act.
